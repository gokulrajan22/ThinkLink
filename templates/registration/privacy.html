Privacy Notice

Policy version {{ policy_version }} · Last updated 26 October 2026

This notice explains what ThinkLink's student collaboration prototype processes. ThinkLink is not an educational institution and does not verify a student's identity, skill level, or project contributions.

Information collected

Account username, email address, password hash, name, college/semester, interests, availability, contribution preferences, self-reported skills, project briefs, generated team-analysis results, consent version/time, and basic security counters. Passwords are never stored in plaintext.

How information is used

Profile information and a project brief are used to produce skill-coverage analysis. If the optional AI provider is configured, the project brief (not account password) may be sent to Groq to extract likely requirements. Provider processing is subject to its own terms and privacy practices. Rule-based analysis is used when AI is not configured or fails.

Optional GitHub inspection

GitHub inspection is user-initiated and optional. It reads public GitHub user/repository metadata and a bounded sample of public source files. ThinkLink never executes repository code. Results are weak signals, not proof of authorship or proficiency. Findings are saved to your profile only when you explicitly choose your own profile; you can remove saved GitHub evidence in profile settings.

Visibility and sharing

Once your email is verified, your profile (name, college, semester, role, skills, interests, availability) appears in the student directory for other signed-in students, so they can message you or invite you to a team. You can hide yourself at any time under "Show me in the student directory" in profile settings; people who already share a project with you can still message you. Saved analyses are private unless you open a project to join requests, which shows its title, brief, and missing skills to signed-in students. Do not enter sensitive personal information, confidential project details, credentials, or secrets in project briefs or messages.

Messages, invites, and notifications

Direct messages and team chats are stored so participants can read them; they are visible only to the people in that conversation. Team invites, join requests, and notifications are stored with your account. By default ThinkLink emails you about invites, join requests, and new messages (at most one message email per conversation per hour); you can turn emails off in profile settings. Your data export includes messages you sent, invites, memberships, and notifications. Deleting your account deletes the messages you sent.

Retention and control

Profile data and saved analyses are retained while your account exists. You can correct profile fields, export account/profile/analysis data as JSON, remove saved GitHub evidence, or delete your account and linked profile/analyses. Security counters are kept for abuse prevention and aged out by maintenance. Operational backups may retain deleted data until their provider-defined expiry; do not assume deletion from backups is immediate.

Security and email

Passwords are hashed by Django. Email verification is required before sign-in. Password reset links are sent to the account email. Security logs record rate-limit events with hashed IP indicators, not raw IP addresses. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and contact

Registration requires consent to this notice for the stated features. You can stop using ThinkLink and delete your account at any time. For privacy questions or data correction issues, contact the project maintainer through the repository's official contact channel once published.
